About Us

Building Discipline, Strength & Confidence Through Martial Arts

Manabu Shotokan Sports Karate Do Association India is dedicated to promoting traditional martial arts with a focus on discipline, respect, and personal growth. Our academy provides professional training in Karate, Judo, Koshiki, Silambam, and Yoga for students of all age groups.

Under the expert guidance of experienced instructors, we aim to develop not only physical strength but also mental focus and strong character. Our training programs are designed to enhance self-confidence, coordination, and overall fitness.

  • Professional Training

    Expert coaching with structured programs for beginners to advanced levels.

  • Self Defense Skills

    Learn practical techniques to protect yourself with confidence.

  • Physical Fitness

    Improve strength, flexibility, endurance, and overall health.

  • Character Development

    Build discipline, respect, patience, and leadership qualities.
