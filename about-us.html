About MSSKAI

Building Discipline, Strength & Champions Through Martial Arts

Manabu Shotokan Sports Karate Do Association India (MSSKAI) is dedicated to empowering students through professional martial arts training. We provide expert coaching in Karate, Silambam, and Archery while helping students develop discipline, confidence, self-defense skills, and physical fitness.

Under the guidance of experienced instructors, our academy prepares students for national competitions, belt grading programs, demonstrations, and personal growth.

  • Professional Karate Training
  • Traditional Silambam Classes
  • Advanced Archery Coaching
  • Self Defense Programs
  • National Tournament Preparation
  • Belt Grading Certification
  • Expert Trainers
  • Fitness & Discipline Development
