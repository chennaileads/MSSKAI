Karate Course Details

Master Discipline, Strength & Self Defense

At MSSKAI, we provide professional karate training programs for students of all ages. Our training focuses on self-defense, discipline, fitness, confidence building, tournament preparation, and belt grading excellence.

Students receive expert guidance from certified instructors and participate in national-level tournaments, demonstrations, and grading examinations.

What You'll Learn

  • Basic Karate Techniques
  • Self Defense Training
  • Kata & Kumite Practice
  • Weapon Training Basics
  • Tournament Preparation
  • Fitness & Flexibility Training

Belt Grading Program

Our academy follows a structured grading system from beginner belts to advanced Black Belt levels including:

  • White Belt
  • Yellow Belt
  • Orange Belt
  • Green Belt
  • Brown Belt
  • Black Belt (Dan Levels)
